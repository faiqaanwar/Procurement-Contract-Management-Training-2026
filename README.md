# Procurement & Contract Management Training 2026

## Project Overview
A five-day professional training program on procurement and contract management, conducted by CUI in collaboration with PPRA.

## My Role
Managed program planning, participant communication, logistics, documentation, feedback collection, and post-training reporting.

## Objectives
To strengthen participants' knowledge of procurement regulations, contract management, compliance, transparency, and E-PADS 2.0.

## Data & Feedback Analysis
Consolidated and analyzed participant feedback collected through daily Google Forms to assess training effectiveness and participant experience.

## Key Findings
Identified participant satisfaction, learning outcomes, trainer effectiveness, and areas for improvement based on the consolidated feedback.
